背景介紹 Biography

關於王三源老師 About Dr. San-Yuan Wang

王三源博士畢業於國立臺灣大學資訊工程學研究所，擁有扎實的演算法設計、軟體工程與機器學習底蘊。

加入臺北醫學大學後，王老師將資訊專長與臨床醫學、藥物科學深度交織，專注於計算代謝體學（Computational Metabolomics）、計算質譜學（Computational Mass Spectrometry）與生醫訊號/大數據分析。他研發出如 Batch normalizer 等具國際影響力的質譜數據校正演算法，顯著降低複雜生醫實驗中的批次效應，並廣泛應用於代謝體學與轉譯醫學研究。其開創性研究成果深獲肯定，曾榮獲國際代謝體學學會第一屆青年學者獎（Metabolomics Society 2015 Early Career Award）及國家科學及技術委員會（國科會）104 年度博士後研究人員學術研究獎。

在教學創新上，王老師深信「以人為本」才是培育未來人才的核心。身為教育部苗圃計畫之授證教練，他善用設計思考（Design Thinking）的養分深入理解同學的學習痛點與需求，據以精心規劃課程架構，引導跨領域學生在生醫與資訊之間融會貫通。其教學熱忱備受肯定，連續榮獲臺北醫學大學 113 學年度「典範課程獎」以及 114 學年度「校級教學優良教師」殊榮。

Dr. San-Yuan Wang received his Ph.D. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from National Taiwan University, with deep expertise in algorithm design, data engineering, and machine learning.

Upon joining Taipei Medical University, he bridged computer science with clinical pharmacology and medicine, specializing in Computational Metabolomics, Computational Mass Spectrometry, and Biomedical Data Science. He developed internationally adopted methods such as Batch normalizer to adjust for batch and injection order effects in LC-MS, widely applied in metabolomics and translational medicine research. His groundbreaking contributions were internationally and nationally recognized with the inaugural Metabolomics Society 2015 Early Career Award and the NSTC Postdoctoral Academic Research Award.

In pedagogical innovation, Dr. Wang believes that empathy is foundational to empowering future talents. As a certified coach of the MOE Design Thinking Nursery Project, he draws on the principles of Design Thinking to empathize with students' learning curves and design learner-centric curricula, bridging biomedical science and computational technology. His dedicated teaching excellence was celebrated consecutively with the TMU Exemplary Course Award (AY2024–25) and the TMU University-Level Excellent Teaching Award (AY2025–26).

核心領域 Key Expertise

研究主軸與學術專長 Research Thrusts & Focus Areas

計算代謝體學 & 計算質譜學 Computational Metabolomics & Mass Spectrometry

專注於液相層析串聯質譜（LC-MS/MS）數據前處理與訊號解析。研發代謝物分子滯留時間（Retention Time）預測演算法、非標靶代謝物注釋及高效批次效應校正方法（如 Batch normalizer）。

Focusing on LC-MS/MS data processing and deconvolution. Developing machine learning retention time prediction, untargeted metabolite annotation, and robust batch correction tools (e.g., Batch normalizer).

LC-MS/MS Batch Correction Retention Time Feature Extraction

智慧醫療與臨床預後預測 Intelligent Healthcare & Patient Prognosis Prediction

將進階機器學習與臨床真實世界大數據（EHR）結合，專注於病人疾病預後評估與不良反應風險預測。例如開發多中心回溯性模型，精準預測抗生素引發之急性腎損傷（AKI）臨床風險，提供個人化臨床決策支援。

Coupling machine learning algorithms with clinical EHR data for patient prognosis prediction and risk stratification. Developed multicenter models predicting antibiotic-associated acute kidney injury (AKI) to guide individualized clinical decisions.

Prognosis Prediction Clinical AI Risk Stratification Decision Support

生物標記物發掘與轉譯體學 Biomarker Discovery & Translational Omics

運用多體學整合分析（Proteomics、Lipidomics、Glycomics）進行關鍵生物標記物發掘（Biomarker Discovery）。例如解析免疫球蛋白 G（IgG）醣基化圖譜以高精準度鑑別自體免疫胰臟炎與胰臟癌，並探究腸道菌相與宿主代謝之調控機制。

Employing multi-omics integration (proteomics, lipidomics, glycomics) for high-impact biomarker discovery. Discovered IgG glycosylation signatures to distinguish autoimmune pancreatitis from pancreatic cancer with high diagnostic accuracy.

Biomarker Discovery Glycomics Multi-omics Translational Medicine

藥物分析與化學指紋圖譜 Pharmaceutical Analysis & Fingerprinting

結合分析化學技術與資料科學，研發柱後衍生化增敏技術，有效提升羥基代謝物之偵測靈敏度；並以機器學習整合化學成分與生物活性，建立天然產物（如桑葉）之特徵化指紋圖譜。

Coupling analytical chemistry workflows with data science. Innovated post-column derivatization to boost hydroxyl metabolite detection, and built ML-powered bioactive chemical fingerprinting for natural products.

Derivatization Fingerprinting HPLC / QqQ-MS Natural Products
